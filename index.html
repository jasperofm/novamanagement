FAQ

Questions creators ask us.

Still unsure? Apply anyway and ask us on your strategy call.

Do I need to be making money already?

No. We work with creators and models at every level, from brand new pages to established accounts that want to break into the top 0.1%. Your plan is built around where you are today.

What is the difference between hybrid and full management?

With the hybrid model you keep control of some parts of your page, like posting or certain chats, while we handle the rest. With full management we run the whole operation and you focus only on creating.

Who chats with my fans?

Our trained in-house chatting team, working in shifts so your page is covered 24/7. They learn your voice, your limits and your fans before they start.

How do you get paid?

We work on a revenue share, so we only earn when you earn. We go over the exact split on your strategy call.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your details are only used by our team to contact you about your application. We never share them, and we sign an agreement before handling any of your accounts.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually within 24 hours, on the contact method you pick in your application: WhatsApp, iMessage or email.
